## 3. ADVANCED METHODOLOGIST PERSPECTIVE

### V1 Assessment (May 2025) - 4.0/5

**Statistical Assessment:**
- Core methods accurate ✅
- Major methodological gaps ⚠️
- 5 critical red flags 🔴
- 6 warnings 🟡
- **Verdict:** "Good foundation but needs significant development"

**RED FLAGS (Critical Issues):**
1. 🔴 No Hartung-Knapp adjustment option
2. 🔴 Continuity correction hardcoded at 0.5
3. 🔴 Only DerSimonian-Laird τ² estimator
4. 🔴 No sensitivity to estimator choice
5. 🔴 Prediction intervals not displayed by default

**WARNINGS (Important Limitations):**
1. 🟡 Limited publication bias methods (only Egger, funnel)
2. 🟡 No Bayesian meta-analysis
3. 🟡 No multivariate meta-analysis
4. 🟡 Meta-regression limited
5. 🟡 Diagnostics could be more comprehensive
6. 🟡 Rare events handling suboptimal

**Benchmark Validation:**
- 10/10 published meta-analyses reproduced exactly ✅
- Computational accuracy perfect ✅
- Methods implemented correctly (where present) ✅
