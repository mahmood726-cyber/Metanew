   - Clinical trials: 90% conducted in high-income countries (US, EU)

   - Collecting WHO/World Bank data: 8-10 hours manual work (user's pain point)
   - Data format inconsistencies (CSV, Excel, PDF)
   - **Problem:** "I find that data supermessy" (user quote)

3. **Budget Constraints:**
   - LMIC researchers: Limited funding (<£5,000/year for software)
   - RevMan (free) but limited; Stata (£1,200/year) unaffordable
   - **Problem:** Need professional tools but can't afford commercial pricing

4. **HTA for Resource-Constrained Settings:**
   - NICE methods don't apply (£20-30k/QALY threshold vs £1-3k in LMICs)
   - Different costing (informal care, traditional medicine)
   - **Problem:** No tools designed for LMIC HTA
   - **EvidenceOS Solution:** LMIC-specific templates (WHO-CHOICE, iDSI methods) ✅

- Large budgets (>£100M/year programs)

## 8. Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Low Adoption in LMICs

**Risk:** LMIC researchers don't adopt due to unfamiliarity with advanced tools

**Mitigation:**
- Free training webinars (monthly, in different time zones)
- Partnership with universities (integrate into courses)
- Freemium option: Limited free tier for students (5 analyses/year)

### Risk 2: Pricing Too High for Individuals

**Risk:** £900/year still unaffordable for PhD students

**Mitigation:**
- Student discount (additional 25% off): £675/year
- Monthly payment option (£75/month, easier to budget)
- University partnerships (supervisor pays from grant)
- Apply for Gates Foundation "Digital Public Goods" grant (offer free licenses to 100 students/year)

### Risk 3: Data Quality Issues

**Risk:** WHO/World Bank data has gaps/errors, users lose trust

**Mitigation:**
- Transparency: Show data source and last update date
- Data quality flags: Mark incomplete/outdated data
- Manual override: Allow users to input their own data if WHO/WB data unavailable
- Regular validation: Quarterly checks against official WHO/WB websites

### Risk 4: Competitive Response

**Risk:** RevMan or CMA adds similar features (free)

**Mitigation:**
- First-mover advantage (12-18 month lead time)
- Network effects (user-generated template library creates lock-in)
- Continuous innovation (add IHME GBD, parametric survival, MASEM in Year 2-3)
- Brand positioning: "Purpose-built for global health" (not an add-on feature)

### Risk 5: Limited Market Size

**Risk:** Overestimated TAM, fewer customers than projected

**Mitigation:**
- Conservative projections (10% of addressable market)
- Expand to high-income researchers doing global health work (secondary market)
- Pivot to "International Edition" if pure LMIC focus too narrow
- Monitor Year 1 closely: If <20 customers by Q4, reassess pricing/positioning
