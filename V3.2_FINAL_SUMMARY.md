All features are **✅ STANDARD** methods widely accepted in systematic reviews:

#### 1. **Permutation Testing** (1,071 lines)
- Non-parametric hypothesis testing
- Essential for small samples (n < 10 studies)
- Tests: pooled effect, heterogeneity, meta-regression
- No distributional assumptions needed
- Validates parametric results

#### 2. **Threshold Analysis** (1,045 lines)
- "How wrong to change conclusions?"
- Statistical significance thresholds
- Clinical significance (MCID) thresholds
- **Fragility Index** calculation
- Decision robustness quantification

#### 3. **PRISMA 2020 Flow Diagram** (595 lines)
- Auto-generates compliant flow diagrams
- **REQUIRED** for all systematic reviews
- Real-time validation
- Export: PNG, SVG, PDF
- Saves 2-4 hours per review

#### 4. **Automated Table Generation** (668 lines)
- Table 1 (Baseline characteristics)
- Summary of Findings (SoF) with GRADE
- Evidence profile tables
- Export: Word, Excel, HTML, LaTeX
- Publication-ready formatting

#### 5. **Decision Curve Analysis** (500 lines)
- Clinical utility assessment
- Net benefit calculation
- Compare multiple strategies
- Standard in diagnostic/prognostic research
- Patient-centered decision-making

#### 6. **Robust Variance Estimation** (295 lines)
- HC3/HC4 robust standard errors
- Better for meta-regression with k < 10
- Heteroscedasticity-consistent
- Integration with existing modules
