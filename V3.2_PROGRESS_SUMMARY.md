## ✅ COMPLETED FEATURES (Part 1)

### 1. Permutation Testing for Meta-Analysis
**File:** `frontend/modules/permutation_tests.R`
**Lines:** 1,071
**Status:** ✅ COMPLETE & TESTED
**Type:** STANDARD methodology

**Capabilities:**
- Permutation test for pooled effect (10,000+ permutations)
- Permutation test for heterogeneity (Q & I²)
- Permutation test for meta-regression coefficients
- Parametric vs non-parametric p-value comparison
- Permutation distribution visualization
- Convergence diagnostics
- Agreement assessment and interpretation

**Why Important:**
- Essential for small samples (n < 10 studies)
- No distributional assumptions
- Validates parametric results
- Robust to outliers and skewness
- Provides exact p-values

**Clinical Use Cases:**
- Small meta-analyses where normality assumptions fail
- Validation of borderline significant results
- Regulatory submissions requiring robust methods
- Meta-analyses with extreme outliers

**Why Important:**

- Transparent sensitivity analysis
- Regulatory requirement (increasingly)

**Clinical Use Cases:**
- HTA submissions to NICE, CADTH, PBAC
- Guideline development
- High-stakes clinical decisions
- Borderline significance assessment
- Fragility assessment for publication

**Fragility Index Guidelines:**
- < 3: Fragile (caution needed)
- 3-10: Moderate robustness
- > 10: Robust findings
