### 1. Budget Impact Analysis (BIA) (638 lines)
**Purpose:** 5-year budget projections for reimbursement decisions

**Key Capabilities:**

**Population Projections:**
- Baseline eligible population with annual growth
- Automatic population projection over time horizon
- Configurable growth rates

**Market Uptake Modeling:**
- S-curve (logistic) uptake model - realistic adoption pattern
- Linear uptake model - steady growth
- Configurable maximum market share
- Adjustable uptake speed

**Cost Calculations:**
- Treatment costs (current vs new therapy)
- Administration costs
- Monitoring costs
- Total cost per patient calculations

**Budget Impact Formula:**
```
Budget Impact = Cost_New_Scenario - Cost_Current_Scenario

Where:
Cost_New_Scenario = (Patients_New × Cost_New) + (Patients_Current × Cost_Current)
Cost_Current_Scenario = Total_Population × Cost_Current
```

**Sensitivity Analyses:**
- **One-Way Sensitivity:** Varies each parameter individually
  - Cost of new therapy
  - Market share
  - Population growth rate
- **Scenario Analysis:** Pessimistic, Realistic, Optimistic
  - Pessimistic: 50% lower share, 20% higher cost, 50% lower growth
  - Realistic: Base case
  - Optimistic: 50% higher share, 10% lower cost, 50% higher growth

**Visualizations:**
1. **Annual Budget Impact:** Line chart showing year-by-year impact
2. **Cumulative Budget Impact:** Area chart showing total impact
3. **Scenario Comparison:** Grouped bar chart across scenarios
4. **Tornado Diagram:** One-way sensitivity analysis

**Excel Export:**
- Complete results in multiple sheets
- Base case details
- Scenario analysis
- Sensitivity analysis
- Editable template for customization

**Impact:** Addresses #1 requirement for NICE, CADTH, PBAC submissions

### 2. Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) (699 lines)
**Purpose:** Systematic decision-making across multiple criteria and stakeholders

**Key Capabilities:**

**MCDA Framework:**
- Weighted scoring across multiple criteria
- Flexible number of alternatives (treatments)
- Customizable criteria selection

**Standard Criteria Domains:**
1. **Clinical Effectiveness:**
   - Mortality reduction
   - Quality of life improvement

2. **Safety:**
   - Adverse events (lower is better)

3. **Economic:**
   - Cost-effectiveness (ICER - lower is better)
   - Budget impact (lower is better)

4. **Implementation:**
   - Ease of use

5. **Equity & Ethical:**
   - Equity of access

**Scoring:**
- 0-100 scale for all criteria
- Automatic normalization based on best/worst performers
- Higher-is-better or lower-is-better for each criterion

**Weighting Methods:**
1. **Equal Weights:** All criteria weighted equally (1/n each)
2. **Direct Weighting:** User assigns weights directly (sum to 100%)
3. **Swing Weighting:** Importance ratings converted to weights

**MCDA Score Calculation:**
```
Overall_Score = Σ (Criterion_Score_i × Weight_i)
```

**Visualizations:**
1. **Radar/Spider Chart:** Performance profile across all criteria
   - Shows strengths and weaknesses
   - Larger area = better overall performance

2. **Stacked Bar Chart:** Contribution of each criterion to overall score
   - Shows which criteria drive the decision
   - Ordered by overall score

3. **Overall Scores Bar Chart:** Simple comparison of alternatives

4. **Tornado Diagram (Weight Sensitivity):**
   - Shows impact of ±25% change in each criterion weight
   - Identifies most influential criteria
   - Strategy-specific sensitivity

**Sensitivity Analysis:**
- Varies each criterion weight by ±25%
- Renormalizes other weights proportionally
- Shows impact on overall scores
- Identifies robust vs fragile decisions

**Use Cases:**
- HTA committee decisions
- Formulary committees
- Multi-stakeholder decision-making
- Value frameworks (ICER, ESMO, ASCO)

**Impact:** Implements NICE 2022 methods update on MCDA

### 3. PSA Dashboard Enhancement (541 lines)
**Purpose:** Advanced visualizations and decision support for probabilistic analysis

**Key Capabilities:**

**Cost-Effectiveness Acceptability Frontier (CEAF):**
- Shows which strategy has highest probability of being cost-effective at each WTP threshold
- Formula: For each WTP, max(Pr[Cost-Effective]) across all strategies
- Identifies optimal strategy across threshold range
- Clearer than overlapping CEAC curves

**Expected Loss Curves:**
- **Purpose:** Expected opportunity cost of choosing each strategy
- **Formula:** E[max(NMB) - NMB_strategy]
- **Interpretation:** Lower expected loss = better choice
- Shows financial risk of being wrong
- Complements CEA probabilities with loss magnitude

**Incremental NMB (INMB) Distribution:**
- Histogram/density of INMB between two strategies
- Shows full uncertainty distribution
- Probability that INMB > $0 = probability cost-effective
- Visualizes decision uncertainty

**Enhanced Cost-Effectiveness Plane:**
- **95% Confidence Ellipse:** Shows joint uncertainty in costs and effects
- **WTP Threshold Ray:** Shows cost-effectiveness threshold
- **Quadrant Probabilities:**
  - NE (Dominant): Effect+ Cost- → Always adopt
  - SE (Trade-off): Effect+ Cost+ → Depends on WTP
  - SW (Dominated): Effect- Cost+ → Never adopt
  - NW (Trade-off): Effect- Cost- → Depends on WTP
- Mean point clearly marked
- Interactive zooming

**Interactive Features:**
- **WTP Threshold Slider:**
  - Drag to change willingness-to-pay
  - All visualizations update in real-time
  - Range: $0 to $200,000
  - Shows impact of threshold on decisions

- **Strategy Selector:**
  - Toggle strategies on/off
  - Compare subsets of strategies
  - Focus on relevant comparisons

**Population EVPI Calculator:**
- Scales per-patient EVPI to population level
- Inputs:
  - Annual incidence (new patients/year)
  - Time horizon (typically 10 years)
  - Discount rate (typically 3.5%)
- **Formula:**
  ```
  Population_EVPI = Σ (EVPI_per_patient × Incidence × Discount_Factor)
  ```
- Shows total value of eliminating uncertainty
- Informs research funding decisions

**Outputs:**
- High-resolution plots for publications
- Interactive HTML dashboard
- Quick stats at selected WTP threshold

**Impact:** Transforms existing BCEA integration into comprehensive decision support tool
