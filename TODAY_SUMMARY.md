## 🎯 STRATEGIC INSIGHTS

### LLMs are PERFECT for:
✅ Risk of Bias (complex reasoning)
✅ PDF Extraction (vision, layouts)
✅ NLQ Parsing (already working great)
✅ Report Generation (text generation)

### ML is PERFECT for:
✅ Study Screening (high volume, 10K+ abstracts)
